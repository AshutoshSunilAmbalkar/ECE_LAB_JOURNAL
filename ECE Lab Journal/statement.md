Problem Statement:
In an Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) lab, the management of 
equipment, ranging from microcontrollers to measuring devices, typically involves a
manual logbook. This system has several issues related to human error, loss of 
information, and searchability and requires a streamlined digital solution to record and view the inventory of available equipment.


Scope of the project:
The current iteration of this project is focused on the simplest aspects of inventory management: entry and access.
This application only allows the appending of new equipment and the full viewing of the inventory using a local file.
